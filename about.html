The Studio

An Atelier Inside
a Hardware Company

CSJ began as a family business in zinc alloy die-casting, with our own mold workshop and casting floor. When a jewelry designer joined the family, the two worlds found each other: the sensitivity of fine jewelry design, applied to the hardware every garment carries.

Today we develop signature zipper pulls and sliders for brands that care about the details their customers touch every day.

The Designer

Trained in Jewelry,
Devoted to Hardware

Our lead designer trained and practiced in fine jewelry design before turning to garment hardware. She brings a jeweler's discipline to proportion, weight, finish and light - treating every zipper pull as a small wearable piece.

“The details are not the details. They make the product.”
